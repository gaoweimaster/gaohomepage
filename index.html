Success Cases

Sumisho International Logistics Co., Ltd., an international freight forwarder (Class A), has developed a professional-grade inventory management system supporting multi-warehouse, multi-client, and multi-lot management, with real-time inventory monitoring, location optimization, stock alerts, QR code recognition, and complete warehouse workflows. The system supports PDAs operation to enable mobile, real-time, and paperless warehouse operations, significantly improving operational efficiency and data accuracy. It Integrates email automation capabilities into the system to automatically download and parse customer order emails, generate standardized files, and achieve seamless linkage between order processing and inventory management.

Legrand Low-Voltage Electrical Appliances (Wuxi), a world-renowned electrical enterprise, has developed a production line label management system. This system generates a unique label for each switch product. The label includes the product QR code and basic information, thus enabling the traceability management of electrical products and ensuring the accurate tracking of information in all links such as production and circulation.

WUXI AFOST PRECISION MACHINERY MANUFACTURE CO., LTD, a medium-sized foundry enterprise, has developed a comprehensive factory management system. This system deeply integrates the inspection process, comprehensively covering every crucial step from in-process inspection to finished-product inspection, effectively assisting the enterprise in strict quality control. Moreover, this system fully takes into account the high-standard requirements of foreign enterprise customers. Not only does it ensure that product quality meets the standards, but it also provides refined functional support in aspects such as production process management, data recording and analysis, thus comprehensively enhancing the factory's operational efficiency and management level to meet the strict multi-dimensional demands of foreign enterprise customers for supply chain partners in terms of quality and management.

China State Construction Engineering Corporation, an important state-owned backbone enterprise, has developed a construction site management system. Both the permit approval process and construction log during the construction process can be completed on-site via mobile phones.

Linde Gas (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., an advanced gas supplier, has developed a gas cylinder pressure monitoring and management platform. This platform can count the usage situation at customers' sites within 24 hours or 48 hours, predict customers' orders (including product types, quantities, and possible order dates), and promptly notify the business department.

XCMG Kaigong Heavy Industry, a significant player in the large-scale shield machine manufacturing field, has a project management software independently developed by us. This software is equipped with comprehensive management functions, covering overall project planning and management, refined task division, a convenient daily work reporting mechanism, an efficient alarm tracking system, as well as comprehensive project maintenance, etc. It helps the enterprise achieve efficient and precise project control.
